# Tell catalog

Judge clusters rather than single tokens; a lone marker can be ordinary. One em dash means nothing. Em dashes plus rule-of-three plus "vibrant tapestry" in the same passage is a confession.

### Content patterns

| Tell | Watch for | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Significance inflation | stands/serves as, testament, pivotal moment, underscores importance, evolving landscape, indelible mark | State what the thing is or does; cut the commentary |
| Name-dropping | listing outlets/follower counts as proof | Pick one source and say what was said |
| Superficial -ing tails | highlighting..., ensuring..., reflecting..., showcasing... tacked on | Delete, or promote its real information to a sourced sentence |
| Promotional language | nestled, vibrant, breathtaking, must-visit, world-class, rich (figurative) | Replace adjectives with specifics |
| Vague attribution | Experts argue, industry reports suggest, observers have cited | Name the real source or cut; never invent one |
| Formulaic challenges sections | Despite these challenges..., Future Outlook | State specific problems, or cut |
| Generic positive endings | future looks bright, exciting times lie ahead, journey toward excellence | End on the last concrete fact |

### Language patterns

| Tell | Watch for | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI vocabulary | delve, tapestry, leverage, robust, seamless, crucial, pivotal, foster, garner, interplay, realm, showcase, testament, underscore, multifaceted | See tier table below |
| Copula avoidance | serves as, stands as, boasts, features instead of is/has | Just say is or has |
| Negative parallelisms | not just X but Y; It's not merely X, it's Y; Not a X. Not a Y. A Z.; tailing negation fragments such as "no guessing" | State the actual claim or fold the consequence into a complete clause |
| Rule of three | any triplet used for rhythm | Use the natural number; two is underrated |
| Synonym cycling | protagonist / main character / central figure for one entity | Repeat the clearest word |
| False ranges | from X to Y where X and Y are not on a scale | List the actual items |
| Passive and subjectless | mistakes were made, no configuration needed, results are preserved | Name the actor; you beats people |
| False agency | the decision emerges, the data tells us, this ensures | Decisions don't emerge; someone decides |
| Compound modifier clusters | dense runs of generic hyphenated pairs such as cross-functional and client-facing | Keep standard or technical compounds; unpack only a cluster that makes the prose feel assembled |

Tiered vocabulary confidence:

- **Tier 1, always flag:** delve, tapestry (figurative), testament (figurative), underscore (verb), leverage (verb), multifaceted, realm, interplay, it's worth noting, in today's ... landscape
- **Tier 2, flag in density (2+ per paragraph):** crucial, pivotal, vibrant, robust, seamless, foster, enhance, showcase, notably, moreover, furthermore, utilize, align with
- **Tier 3, never flag alone:** key, important, significant, various, effective, valuable. Ordinary words; only evidence alongside Tier 1 or 2 clusters.

### Style patterns

| Tell | Watch for | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Em dashes | any em/en dash as default connector | Period, comma, colon, or parentheses; catch spaced dashes and ` -- ` too |
| Boldface overuse | bold scattered mid-sentence with no rule | Let words do the work |
| Inline-header lists | **Performance:** Performance has been enhanced... | Prose, or a plain list |
| Title case headings | ## Strategic Negotiations And Partnerships | Sentence case |
| Emoji decoration | 🚀 **Launch Phase:** | Remove |
| Excessive structure | headers, tables, nested bullets for content fitting two paragraphs | Collapse into prose; structure follows content |
| Fragmented headers | heading followed by one line restating it | Cut the restating line |
| Diff-anchored writing | docs narrating changes instead of current behavior | Describe the thing as it is (changelogs exempt) |
| Markdown bleeding | **bold** in emails, DMs, plain-text contexts | Strip where it won't render |

### Communication patterns

Chatbot artifacts left in content: I hope this helps, Of course!, Certainly!, Would you like me to, let me know. Knowledge-cutoff disclaimers: as of my last update, based on available information. Speculative gap-fill: information is scarce, suggesting she keeps a low profile; she likely grew up... Sycophancy: Great question! You're absolutely right! Reasoning-chain scaffolding: Let me think, Step 1:, Breaking this down. Signposting: Let's dive in, here's what you need to know, in this section we'll. Acknowledgment loops: restating the question before answering. Citation markup leaks: citeturn0search0, oaicite, utm_source=chatgpt.com, [INSERT SOURCE URL] placeholders. Delete all of these.

### Filler and hedging

Filler phrases collapse mechanically: in order to → to; due to the fact that → because; at this point in time → now; has the ability to → can; it is important to note that X → X. Hedging stacks collapse to one qualifier per claim: "could potentially possibly be argued that it might" → "may". Adverb pileup (genuinely, really, actually, simply, truly, fundamentally) gets cut; if the sentence collapses without the adverb, the sentence was the problem. Do not mechanically delete every adverb: keep one that carries scope, timing, contrast, or the author's voice.

### Rhetoric and cadence

Fake-candid hooks (Honestly?, Here's the thing, Let's be honest, Look,) get cut; a person being honest just says the thing. Persuasive authority tropes (the real question is, at its core, what really matters, the deeper issue) restate ordinary points with ceremony; state the point. Aphorism formulas (X is the Y of Z, the currency of, X becomes a trap) dress claims as sayings; state the concrete claim. Manufactured punchlines stack fragments for drama; one short beat is fine, a run is engineered. Answering objections no one raised (this isn't about X, I'm not saying Y) records earlier drafting arguments; delete unless the objection has a real source. Rejecting fake alternatives (a tempting approach would be...) leaves old drafts in the text; remove the option nobody considered and state the real constraint. Narrator-from-a-distance (nobody designed this, people tend to) floats above the scene; put the reader in the room. Throat-clearing openers (in today's fast-paced world, performance is everything) precede any article on anything; start with the actual claim.

Rhetorical questions answered immediately ("What makes an API good? It comes down to predictability.") add a beat without adding information; state the answer directly unless the question is genuine. Sentence-opener tics such as "So", "Look", "Interestingly", "Importantly", or habitual sentence-initial "And"/"But" should be removed when they announce a point rather than carry meaning; preserve an earned habit that is part of the author's voice. Reassurance kickers ("And that's okay", "you're not alone", "no shame in") comfort a reader who did not ask for reassurance; cut them unless the reassurance is part of the intended content.

### Structural tells

These are how readers spot AI text even when vocabulary is clean:

- Uniform sentence length. Three consecutive sentences of similar length breaks the illusion. Mix 4-word sentences with 30-word ones; humans write in bursts.
- Uniform paragraph length and shape (topic sentence → explanation → example → transition, every time). Break it. Let some paragraphs be one sentence. Let some end abruptly without transition.
- Parataxis chains. Short sentence. Then another. Then another. Connect related thoughts with subordinate clauses, conjunctions, semicolons.
- Hedging seesaw. Pick a side; acknowledge counterpoints in one sentence max.
- Parallel structure across sections and interchangeable paragraphs. Test: can you swap paragraphs 2 and 4 without breaking anything? Then they are padding; make each depend on the last.
- Paragraph-ending whether summaries ("Whether you're a founder or creator...") and miniature slogans closing each paragraph.
- Bullet lists doing prose work. Make them uneven when used; never more than 5-7 in a row.

Statistical signals behind these rules: burstiness (sentence-length variance) low in AI, high in humans; vocabulary diversity lower in AI; trigram repetition higher in AI; paragraph sizes uniform in AI. Fix them directly by varying lengths, repeating the clearest word instead of cycling synonyms, and varying paragraph size.
